Quadratic Equations & Discriminants

READY
A quadratic equation takes the standard form ax squared plus bx plus c equals zero, where a is not equal to zero. The discriminant, calculated as b squared minus 4ac, determines the nature of the roots. When the discriminant is positive, there are two distinct real solutions. When it equals zero, there is exactly one repeated real root. When negative, the solutions are complex conjugates.
